How the model works
  • Market size = base market × (1 + real growth) × (1 + inflation × price pass-through).
  • Company premium = market size × your market share (share held constant).
  • Claims costs rise with inflation, plus the depreciation on the import-linked share of claims. Premium rates rise only by inflation × pass-through, so the gap moves the loss ratio.
  • Expenses rise with inflation and with half of the volume growth.
  • Investable assets grow with premium; investment income = assets × (base yield + interest rate change).
  • The tornado chart shows each factor's effect on profit before tax when it moves alone.

A teaching model with simplified, transparent assumptions; not a forecast.
