Sensitivity Lab: what will next year's sales be?

Enter this year's sales, then set your expectations for the economy. The app builds next year's expected sales step by step.

1. This year (EGP millions)

2. Your expectations for next year

3. Expected sales next year

Enter this year's sales to see the result.

How the calculation works
  1. Start from this year's sales.
  2. Economic growth: sales × (1 + real GDP growth × the growth rule of thumb).
  3. Inflation: × (1 + inflation × the share passed into premiums).
  4. Pound depreciation: motor, marine cargo, engineering and property rise by the pound's fall × the pass-through share, because the cars, cargo and machinery they insure are priced in dollars. Other lines do not.
  5. Interest rates: each 1-point rise cuts sales by the sensitivity you set; a cut adds to sales.

A simple teaching model, not a forecast. Market share is assumed unchanged.

    Activity 4.4: RACI Builder

    Clarify who owns what in DCI's medical pre-approval automation. For each activity, choose a letter for each role. Rule of thumb: exactly one A and at least one R in every row.

    RResponsibleDoes the work. The person or team that carries out the task. There can be more than one R.
    AAccountableOwns the result and signs it off. The one person who answers for it if it fails. Only one A per activity.
    CConsultedGives input before the work is done or a decision is made. Two-way communication.
    IInformedKept up to date on progress or decisions. One-way communication; no input needed.
    A/RAccountable and responsibleThe same person owns the result and also does the work.
    Edit any activity name, remove rows with ×, or add your own (up to 15). Leave a cell blank if the role has no part in that activity.
