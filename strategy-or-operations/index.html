Sensitivity Lab: what will next year's sales be?

Enter this year's sales, then set your expectations for the economy. The app builds next year's expected sales step by step.

1. This year (EGP millions)

2. Your expectations for next year

3. Expected sales next year

Enter this year's sales to see the result.

How the calculation works
  1. Start from this year's sales.
  2. Economic growth: sales × (1 + real GDP growth × the growth rule of thumb).
  3. Inflation: × (1 + inflation × the share passed into premiums).
  4. Pound depreciation: motor, marine cargo, engineering and property rise by the pound's fall × the pass-through share, because the cars, cargo and machinery they insure are priced in dollars. Other lines do not.
  5. Interest rates: each 1-point rise cuts sales by the sensitivity you set; a cut adds to sales.

A simple teaching model, not a forecast. Market share is assumed unchanged.
